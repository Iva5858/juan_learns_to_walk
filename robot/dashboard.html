Calibration

  1. Zero. Relax all, hold both legs perfectly straight (a jig or a flat wall helps), then Every angle on this page is the simulator's: legs straight = 0°.
  2. Directions. On each joint, torque on and press +5°. It must move the way the card says (e.g. "+ = knee bends"). If it goes the other way, press Flip on that card.
  3. Limits. Relax a joint, move it by hand to where it touches something (another part, a cable, its own bracket), back off a few degrees, and press Lower here / Upper here. Until both are set, a joint uses the simulator's range.
  4. Store in the servos. The dashboard clamps every move to the limits either way; this also protects against other programs.
  5. IMU. Torso axes from sensor axes: I2C bus Check: tip the robot forward → "lean forward" goes up; tip it to its right → "lean right" goes up. Gyro, turning it quickly: rolling right → x positive; pitching nose down → y positive; turning left (counter-clockwise from above) → z positive. Robot still: (also done at start-up). Stand it level and press (display only).
